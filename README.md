
# ASCEND – Kudu Gas Field Case Study

## Project Overview

This project was developed as part of the **ASCEND – Petroleum Track Competition**, organized by the AAPG Ain Shams University Student Chapter.

The case study focuses on evaluating the development opportunities, economic feasibility, financial risks, and commercial challenges associated with the **Kudu Gas Field, offshore Namibia**.

## My Role

**Business Support Monitor**

As a Business Information Systems student, my contribution focused on the business, economic, and financial evaluation of the project.

## My Contributions

- **Market Assessment:** Evaluating domestic energy demand, import substitution opportunities, and gas development scenarios.
- **Risk Assessment:** Analyzing capital expenditure, subsurface uncertainty, licensing challenges, and commercial risks.
- **Financial Analysis:** Evaluating CAPEX, NPV, break-even prices, and financial scenarios.
- **Strategic Recommendations:** Assessing phased development options and investment conditions.

## Key Areas of Analysis

- Domestic Gas-to-Power Opportunities
- LNG Export Feasibility
- Development Scenario Comparison
- Capital Expenditure (CAPEX)
- Net Present Value (NPV)
- Break-even Price Analysis
- Technical and Commercial Risk Assessment
- Investment Strategy and Recommendations

## Main Recommendation

The analysis recommended deferring the Final Investment Decision (FID) and proceeding through staged development gates, subject to improved commercial terms, cost reductions, and an adequate licence extension.
